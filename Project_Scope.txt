Project Scope
In Scope: This project will provide a system for foster care organizations to keep track of foster placements by creating and updating placement records (child ↔ foster home/family), tracking placement history (start/end dates, reason for change, prior placements), and tracking the basic capacity per foster home. The application will also maintain the case history by creating a timeline/log of case events(case ID, assigned caseworker, open/close dates, status changes, placement changes, additional notes) and implementing a search/filter. The application also needs to handle caseworker visit schedules by scheduling required visits, updating the status of the visit(complete/missed) with notes, and implementing a calendar or list view of upcoming/overdue visits per caseworker. The application will track regulatory deadlines, flag/alert when a deadline is approaching or missed, and implement a compliance dashboard as well (% on-track vs. overdue). It will also need a supporting infrastructure for user accounts/roles(caseworker, supervisor/admin, etc.) and basic authentication and access control.
Out of Scope: The application will not implement legal and judicial processes including court filing, e-filing, or docket management; and legal document drafting/generation(petitions, court orders). This will also not implement licensing and background checks including foster parent licensing workflow, background check processing, and training/certification tracking for foster parents. In addition, the application will not include the following: financial systems, state/federal reporting systems, cross-agency/interstate data sharing, communication infrastructure, predictive/ML risk modeling, public-facing components, or direct integration ownership.

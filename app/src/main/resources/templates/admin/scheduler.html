Alert rules

  • An alert is sent only when Deadline.daysRemaining is 7, 1, or 0. No alert on any other day.
  • Deadlines already met produce no alert.
  • Past-due deadlines (Deadline.isOverdue) are flagged "Overdue" instead of getting a reminder.
  • Alerts are shown inside the system only (Alert.send()). No email or push notifications.
